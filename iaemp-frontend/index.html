The Indian Association of Energy Management Professionals (IAEMP) is a national professional body dedicated to promoting energy efficiency, renewable energy adoption, and sustainable energy practices across India. IAEMP brings together energy managers, auditors, engineers, consultants, academicians, and young professionals to collectively contribute to India’s clean energy transition through responsible and informed energy management.

Who We Are

IAEMP is a non-profit professional association committed to advancing the science and practice of energy management. Through knowledge sharing, training, advocacy, and collaboration, IAEMP supports India’s national energy efficiency and sustainability goals.

What We Do

  • Professional training and capacity building
  • National & regional energy awareness programs
  • Technical publications and research
  • Policy and regulatory engagement
  • Industry, institutional & student outreach

Why IAEMP Matters

Energy efficiency and sustainable energy systems are critical to India’s economic growth, energy security, and environmental responsibility. IAEMP empowers professionals with the knowledge, tools, and networks required to create measurable and long-term impact in the energy sector.

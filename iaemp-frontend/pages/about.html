About IAEMP

The Indian Association of Energy Management Professionals (IAEMP) is a national professional body dedicated to promoting energy efficiency, renewable energy, and sustainable development across India.

IAEMP brings together energy managers, auditors, engineers, consultants, academicians, and students to work towards building an energy-efficient nation.

Our Vision

To empower India’s energy future through professional excellence, innovation, and collaboration.

Our Mission

  • Promote best practices in energy efficiency and conservation
  • Support renewable energy adoption
  • Conduct training, workshops, and awareness programs
  • Collaborate with industry, academia, and government bodies

Our Objectives

  • Capacity building of energy professionals
  • Knowledge sharing through publications and events
  • Policy advocacy and technical support
  • Student and young professional engagement

IAEMP History & Milestones

2005

IAEMP was established by energy professionals to promote energy efficiency and conservation across India.

2010

Release of the Vision Document aligned with national energy conservation goals.

2018

Expansion of national training programs, seminars, and awareness campaigns.

2024

Digital transformation of IAEMP platforms and stronger industry engagement.
